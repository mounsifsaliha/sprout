Privacy Policy

Sprout: Daily Habit Tracker · by NovaBit Games · Effective October 23, 2026

In short: Sprout keeps your habits on your device. No accounts, no ads and no analytics. If you turn on Google Drive backup, your data goes to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive, and only you can access it. If you use the AI planner (Sprout Pro), what you type is sent to our planning server and to Anthropic to make your plan, and is not stored or used for training.

1. Who we are

Sprout: Daily Habit Tracker ("Sprout", "the app") is published by NovaBit Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. Questions are welcome at mounsifsaliha6@gmail.com.

2. Data you create in the app

Habits, daily check-ins, notes, streak freezes, moods, custom categories, your display name, an optional avatar picture, your settings and your answers to the onboarding quiz (goals, wake-up time, time budget, obstacle) and your last few AI planner requests are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. The quiz answers are used solely to build your starter plan. We never receive this data and cannot see it.

3. Google Drive backup (optional)

You can connect a Google account to back up your Sprout data. When you do:

  • Sprout asks Google for the drive.appdata permission. This grants access only to a hidden, app-specific folder in your Google Drive. Sprout cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Your backup is a compressed copy of the data described in section 2. It is sent directly from your device to Google Drive over an encrypted connection. It never passes through any server operated by us.
  • Backups run automatically when enabled, or when you tap “Back up now”. You can turn backup off or disconnect the account at any time in Settings.
  • To delete the backup, open Google Drive, go to Settings → Manage apps, find Sprout and choose “Delete hidden app data”. Uninstalling Sprout does not delete it, so that you can restore on a new phone.

Sprout’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. AI planner (Sprout Pro, optional)

The AI tab turns what you type into suggested tasks, habits and challenges. It only runs when you tap to plan, and only after you agree to it in the app. When you use it:

  • What is sent: the text you typed; your language, today’s date and the current time; and, if “Plan around my days” is on (you can turn it off in the AI tab’s settings), the titles and times of your unfinished tasks from the past week and the next two weeks, and the names of your habits, so the planner doesn’t double-book you, doesn’t repeat what you have, and can move tasks when you ask it to. Nothing else from your data is sent: no check-ins, notes, moods, name or photo.
  • Where it goes: over an encrypted connection to our planning server (hosted by Railway), which passes it to Anthropic’s Claude API to write the plan and sends the suggestions back to your device. Nothing is added to your app until you tap Add.
  • What is kept: our server does not store or log what you send or receive. It keeps only a running total of the month’s AI cost, and in memory a count of today’s requests per install, tied to a random ID the app creates for this purpose, to enforce the daily limit. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models and keeps it only for a limited time under its commercial terms and privacy policy.
  • Checking access: to make sure requests come from the genuine app and a Pro member, the app sends a Google Play Integrity token and its Google Play purchase token, which our server checks with Google. These identify the purchase, not you.

AI suggestions can be wrong. Check them before relying on them, and don’t use the planner for medical, legal or financial decisions.

5. Android backup and transfer

Android may include Sprout’s data in the device backup tied to your Google account, and may copy it when you transfer to a new phone. This is controlled by your device’s backup settings, not by us.

6. Export and import

You can export your data as a CSV or JSON file at any time. Exported files are yours and go wherever you choose to share or save them. Importing a file only reads it on your device.

7. Purchases

Sprout Pro is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and tells the app only whether a purchase is active. We never receive your name, card details or billing address.

8. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled and shown locally on your device. No notification service outside the device is involved. If you start a free trial of Sprout Pro, the app schedules one local reminder two days before the trial ends.

9. Google Play services

The app uses Google Play’s in-app review and in-app update features. Google may process device information under the Google Privacy Policy.

10. What we do not do

  • No advertising networks.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that send data to third parties.
  • No user accounts, advertising or tracking identifiers, or sale of data.
  • No access to contacts, location, photos (other than the avatar you pick) or other apps.

11. Children

Sprout is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. We keep no personal information on our side, so there is nothing for us to hold.

12. Deleting your data

Clear the app’s storage or uninstall Sprout to remove everything on the device. Remove the Drive backup as described in section 3. The AI planner keeps nothing to delete (section 4). Device backups follow your Android backup settings.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this address with an updated effective date.
