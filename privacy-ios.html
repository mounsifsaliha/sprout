Privacy Policy

Sprout: Daily Habit Tracker for iPhone · by NovaBit Games · Effective November 2, 2026

This policy covers the iOS app. The Android app has its own policy: Privacy Policy (Android).

In short: Sprout keeps your habits on your iPhone. No accounts, no ads and no analytics. If iCloud sync is on, your data is stored in your own iCloud account through Apple, and we cannot access it. If you use Sprout AI (Sprout Pro), what you ask is sent to our AI server and to Anthropic to answer it, and is not stored or used for training. To show how many people are on each challenge, the app anonymously tells our server which challenges you have running.

1. Who we are

Sprout: Daily Habit Tracker ("Sprout", "the app") is published by NovaBit Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. Questions are welcome at mounsif392@gmail.com.

2. Data you create in the app

Habits, daily check-ins, notes, streak freezes, mood and energy check-ins, custom categories, tasks in Plan, focus sessions, challenges (including Savings Jar and My Goal), your display name, an optional avatar picture, your answers to the welcome questions (goals, wake-up time, time per day — used only to suggest habits), what you fill in under “About you” in the AI tab, your recent AI requests and coach chats, and your settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. The Sprout widget reads the same data from that storage on your device. We never receive this data and cannot see it.

The app also keeps a few counters on your device (number of check-ins, install date, when a rating was last requested) only to decide when to show Apple’s rating prompt.

3. iCloud sync

iCloud sync is on by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings → Data & sync. When it is on:

  • Sprout stores the data described in section 2 in Apple’s CloudKit, in the private database of your own iCloud account. It is kept by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy and counts toward your iCloud storage.
  • Only devices signed in to your Apple Account can read it. We have no access to your iCloud data and it never passes through any server operated by us.
  • Sync lets your habits return when you reinstall Sprout or set up a new iPhone.
  • If you turn sync off, new changes stay on that device only. Data already in iCloud is not deleted. You can remove it in your iCloud storage settings.

4. Sprout AI (Sprout Pro, optional)

The AI tab plans your day, week or goal from what you type or say, and the coach answers questions about your habits. Stats can write a weekly review, and a task can get suggested steps. These only run when you ask, and the planner only after you agree to it in the app. When you use them:

  • What is sent:
    • Planner: the text you typed or dictated; your language, today’s date and the current time; and, if “Plan around my days” is on (you can turn it off in the AI tab), the titles and times of your upcoming and unfinished tasks, the names, times and days of your habits, the names of your running challenges, your wake-up and bed times, your answers to the welcome questions (goals, what you’re cutting down on, time a day, biggest obstacle), and what you filled in under “About you”, so the plan fits your days and doesn’t repeat what you have.
    • Coach and weekly review: your messages, and your habits’ names, schedules, reminder times and goals with how often each was done over recent weeks, your streaks, how many mood check-ins you made and how your good and other days went, and the names of your challenges.
    • Suggested steps: the task’s title, note and length, and the steps it already has.
    Nothing else is sent: no check-in notes, mood notes, name or photo.
  • Where it goes: over an encrypted connection to our AI server (hosted by Railway), which passes it to Anthropic’s Claude API to write the answer and sends it back to your device. Nothing is added to or changed in your app until you tap to add or apply it.
  • What is kept: our server does not store or log what you send or receive, unless you report an answer (see below). It keeps only a running total of the month’s AI cost, and in memory a count of today’s requests per install, tied to a random ID the app creates for this purpose, to enforce the daily limit. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models and keeps it only for a limited time under its commercial terms and privacy policy.
  • Feedback and reports: under each plan you can tap 👍 or 👎. A 👍 is only counted. A 👎 lets you report the plan (for example as harmful or wrong): the report, with what you asked and the plan you got, is sent to our server and kept for up to 90 days so we can review it and improve the planner. It is not linked to you; only the random ID above limits how many reports one install can send a day.
  • Checking access: to make sure requests come from the genuine app and a Pro member, the app uses Apple’s App Attest (a key kept in your iPhone’s Secure Enclave, which Apple vouches for) and sends the App Store’s signed record of your Pro purchase, which our server checks with Apple’s certificates. These identify the purchase, not you.
  • Talking instead of typing: dictation uses Apple’s speech recognition. It runs on your iPhone where supported; otherwise Apple processes the audio under the Apple Privacy Policy. Only the resulting text is sent as described above. The microphone is used only while you dictate.

AI answers can be wrong. Check them before relying on them, and don’t use Sprout AI for medical, legal or financial decisions.

5. Challenge counts

Challenges show how many people are doing them (for example “1,240 doing it now”). To count them, the app sends our server, once a day and whenever it changes, the names of the library challenges you have running and the ones you just completed, with a random ID the app creates for this purpose. Nothing else is sent: no name, no progress, no habits. Our server keeps only a scrambled (hashed) form of that ID with the day it was last seen on each challenge, drops it after 7 days without news, and keeps per-challenge totals. The app reads back only the totals, which are the same for everyone. When the app is offline nothing is sent, and the counts are hidden until enough people are on a challenge.

6. iPhone backups

iOS may include Sprout’s data in your iCloud or computer backup of the device. This is controlled by your device’s backup settings, not by us.

7. Export and import

You can export your data as a CSV or JSON file at any time. Exported files are yours and go wherever you choose to share or save them. Restoring a backup file only reads it on your device.

8. Purchases

Sprout Pro is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether a purchase or subscription is active. We never receive your name, Apple Account details, card details or billing address. When you use Sprout AI, the signed purchase record is checked as described in section 4.

9. Offers

To show limited-time offers on the upgrade screen, the app downloads a small public file, promo.json, from this website (hosted on GitHub Pages). The request contains no personal data or identifiers. As with any web request, GitHub can see your IP address under the GitHub Privacy Statement; we receive nothing.

10. Notifications

Reminders, the morning plan, the evening check-in and, if you start a free trial, one reminder before it ends are scheduled and shown locally on your device. Their text is written on your device, and actions such as “Mark done” run on your device. When iCloud sync is on, Apple may send the app a silent signal that new data is ready to sync; it carries none of your habit data.

11. Siri, Shortcuts and widgets

Sprout offers Siri and Shortcuts actions and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. They run on your device using the data in section 2. When you use Siri, your request is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy; we receive nothing.

12. What we do not do

  • No advertising networks.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that send data to third parties.
  • No user accounts, tracking identifiers or sale of data.
  • No access to contacts, location, Apple Health, photos (other than the avatar you pick) or other apps. The microphone is used only while you dictate in the AI tab.

13. Children

Sprout is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

14. Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything on the device. Data synced to iCloud stays in your iCloud account until you remove it from your iCloud storage settings. Sprout AI keeps nothing to delete (section 4). Challenge counts forget an install 7 days after it stops sending (section 5). Device backups follow your iPhone backup settings.

15. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this address and in the app, with an updated effective date.
